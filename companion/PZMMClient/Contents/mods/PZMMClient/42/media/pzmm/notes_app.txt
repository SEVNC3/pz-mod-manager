# 1.4.0
- Thanks to Lyven Ka Vinsxy, GhostyAshes, newbee, Gentle_Hilt and Yagoyuki for recent ideas and bug reports.
- Hold updates on any mod: click the lock on its row and PZMM keeps that version until you click again. Hold this save's mods in Saves does a whole save at once. Steam can't hold a subscribed mod, so PZMM keeps its own copy of the version you have and unsubscribes it, after asking. Thanks yustin on the Workshop page.
- Saving a server's mods is clearer: the button says Save mods to server, a save bar sits at the bottom while you have unsaved changes, and "Leave without saving?" now has Save and leave. Thanks Mittens The Feared on Discord.
- PZMM opens windowed and fits your screen, even after a resolution change or with a monitor unplugged.
