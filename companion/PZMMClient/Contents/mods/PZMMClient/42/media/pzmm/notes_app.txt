# 1.3.0
- Thanks to Lyven Ka Vinsxy, GhostyAshes, newbee, Gentle_Hilt and Yagoyuki for recent ideas and bug reports.
- "Steam is limiting requests" should be rare now. PZMM asks Steam once and shares the answer, and when Steam does say slow down, everything waits together instead of piling on. A repeat Maps scan reads 1 or 2 pages instead of about 59.
- My mods has proper columns: Size, Installed, Last changed and Overwrites. Click a header to sort by it, click again for your own order.
- An eye next to each tickbox hides a mod from the list without turning it off. Handy for the parts of a mod you never touch, like the other presets of a texture pack. Find them again under Hidden.
- The Workshop panel flicks through a mod's pictures with arrows.
- New filters: Added and Updated in the last day, week or month, and Has an update.
- Lots of small things the in-game manager already had: Join on server rows, more player filters, Rename and Delete for saves (it backs the save up first), a right-click menu on sections, more facts in mod details, and an Open mods folder button.
- The in-game manager can hand jobs to the app now: remove a mod, install one by its mod ID, import a share code, run your hosted server and more. The app asks you first for anything that changes your setup.
