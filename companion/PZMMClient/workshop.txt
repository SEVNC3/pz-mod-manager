description=[h1]PZMM - Client[/h1]
description=Connects Project Zomboid to [url=https://github.com/SEVNC3/pz-mod-manager]PZMM[/url], the free mod manager, server browser and server host.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=When you reach the main menu or load into a world, it tells the PZMM app what you're running: the exact game version, the mods the game actually loaded, and whether you're in singleplayer or on a server. The app uses that to check your profile really got applied.
description=
description=This is the first version of the link between the game and the app. More is coming: your PZMM profile and mod updates shown in the main menu, switching profiles from the game, and the full mod list of a server you're joining.
description=
description=[h2]Safe to leave on[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Client only. Servers don't need it, so it never forces a server restart.
description=[*]It doesn't touch your saves.
description=[*]It only writes one small file, Zomboid/Lua/pzmm/game.json, on your own PC. Nothing goes online.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Get the app[/h2]
description=PZMM installs and sorts your mods, keeps profiles, joins modded servers with exactly the right mods, and hosts dedicated servers. Free and open source, Windows and Linux: [url=https://github.com/SEVNC3/pz-mod-manager]github.com/SEVNC3/pz-mod-manager[/url]
