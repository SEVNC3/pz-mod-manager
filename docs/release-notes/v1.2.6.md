A bigger one this time, mostly from playtesting notes. Lots of small annoyances gone, and a few things that should have been there from the start.

- **Batch actions hit every selected mod.** Turn on, turn off, update or remove a whole selection at once, not just the mod you right-clicked.
- **Subscribe from the chips.** The Workshop and Subscribed chips in My mods subscribe and unsubscribe in one click, and the mod shows up a few seconds later without a rescan.
- **Uninstall from Browse Workshop.** Installed cards get an Uninstall button.
- **ZombieBuddy stops asking.** Java mods you turn on in PZMM are allowed in ZombieBuddy when you press Play. Jars with scan warnings, and jars that aren't from the Workshop, still ask.
- **Check setup is its own window.** Problems are sorted by how bad they are (Broken, Risk, Check, Low), each with a fix button next to it. One button fixes all the harmless ones, and It's fine hides one until the mod changes.
- **Launch warnings come with fixes.** Play tells you what's wrong and offers to fix it before the game starts, with far fewer false alarms.
- **Build 41-only mods are marked Broken on Build 42** instead of silently not loading, with Turn off, Uninstall and Find a B42 version. Sorting no longer switches them back on.
- **Project Viewpoint moves you to Build 42**, and switching away puts your old build back.
- **Rescan** picks up mods Steam downloaded outside PZMM.
- **Progress bars everywhere.** The activity bar shows what's running, and opens into a full log you can filter.
- **Download history** lists every install, update, removal and subscription, with reinstall.
- **Choose what the close button does.** Minimise to tray or quit, with Don't ask again. You can change it in Settings.
- **Faster start.** A short splash while PZMM loads Steam data in the background, so the Workshop, server list and news are ready when you get there. Steam answers are cached for a few minutes, so browsing is quicker and hits Steam's rate limit less.
- **What's new** pops up once after an update (this is it).
