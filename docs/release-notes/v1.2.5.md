Sorry about all the updates today. A few things slipped through the cracks after 1.2.0, and I'd rather fix them straight away than leave you stuck with them. This should be the last one for a while: PZMM is in a really good place now, and future updates will be bigger and less often. Time for me to have a rest.

- **New mods are easy to spot.** Mods a profile hasn't seen yet get a **New** tag in My mods, so you can find them even after PZMM files them into a category.
- **New mods stay at the bottom.** In a profile with sections, newly installed mods could end up in the middle of the list, above a freshly made "Other" section. They now stay at the end, switched off.
- **Workshop cards say Installed.** After installing from Browse Workshop, the card now switches to Installed straight away, including for mods that were pulled in as requirements.
