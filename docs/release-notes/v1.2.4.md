- **Subscribing in Steam takes over.** If you subscribe in Steam to a mod PZMM downloaded for you, PZMM now moves its own copy to the Recycle Bin and the mod shows as Subscribed. Steam keeps it updated from then on. Before, PZMM's older copy stayed and the game kept loading it.
- **Browse Workshop sorting at the top.** Sort by, Popular over, Posted and Updated now sit above the results, and searching keeps the sort you picked. Shelf cards on the Workshop home line up.
- **Group by category is one click.** It's a button next to Filters in My mods, and on a mod's right-click menu.
