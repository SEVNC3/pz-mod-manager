Three fixes for 1.2.0.

- **Remove works on mods PZMM didn't install.** Mods sitting in your `Zomboid/mods` folder that PZMM has no record of (added by hand, or by another copy of PZMM) can now be removed. PZMM can't download those again, so they always go to the Recycle Bin. Steam subscriptions are still yours to unsubscribe in Steam.
- **Removing several mods removes all of them.** Right-click **Remove** on a selected mod used to remove only that one. It now removes the whole selection, and the question pops up in the middle of the window.
- **You can see the server list loading.** After you click **List servers** on Home, a bar shows while PZMM asks Steam.
