Thanks to yustin and Mittens The Feared for recent ideas and bug reports.

- **New group** makes a group of just the mods you picked, instead of taking every mod below them. Right-click a mod for **Add to group**, and click a selected mod again to deselect it. Thanks Yagoyuki on Discord.
- Sorted by name, size or date, each mod shows a coloured tag with its group, and **Filters** has a **Group** filter. The column header lines up properly now too. Thanks Yagoyuki on Discord.
- Dragging a mod near the top or bottom of the list scrolls it, since the mouse wheel doesn't work while dragging. Thanks Yagoyuki on Discord.
- Unsubscribed from a mod but Steam hasn't deleted it yet? It shows **Unsubscribed** now, and **Remove** deletes it. PZMM checks with Steam first and never deletes a mod you're still subscribed to.
- **Copy diagnostics** logs how your list loads, so if groups ever go missing after an update, send it before launching the game.
- The in-game manager has all of this too. Update PZMM - Client on the [Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818460225).
