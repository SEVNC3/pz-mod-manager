![Changelog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SEVNC3/pz-mod-manager/main/docs/media/changelog.png)

- Antivirus, a VPN or a work network no longer breaks downloads with "invalid peer certificate". PZMM trusts the same certificates your PC does now, and if something still blocks it, it tells you what to turn off. Thanks Yagoyuki on Discord.
- **Find the broken mod** keeps mods that need each other together. Viewpoint and its add-ons, or a car framework and its cars, get tested as one group, so it can't blame the wrong mod. It also starts with the mods you added last. Thanks Lyven Ka Vinsxy on the Workshop page.
- Mod details show **First seen**, the day the mod landed on your PC, and **Date installed** sorts by it. Thanks GhostyAshes on Discord.
- Writing your mods to a server shows a checklist. Untick what the server shouldn't get, like client-side add-ons, and it's remembered for that server. Mods that need Viewpoint or ZombieBuddy get a "client-side add-on?" tag. Thanks newbee on Discord.
- Workshop pictures that fail to load try again, then say so instead of leaving a blank box. Gentle_Hilt, if you still get blanks, send me what **Copy diagnostics** gives you (Settings > About).
- The in-game manager does the broken mod search, First seen and the server checklist too. Update PZMM - Client on the [Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818460225).

All of this came from people asking. If something bugs you, say so on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/6URUy6mNzu) or the Workshop page and it might be in the next one.
