- **What's new puts updates you can read first.** Mods whose updates have change notes or a changelog fill the scraps of paper and the top of the history. Updates with nothing to read go to the bottom, under No change notes. Same in the app and in game.
- **Two rows of notes in game.** The in-game What's new board keeps to two rows of scraps; the rest are in the history below.
- **We're sorry, in game.** Without the app, the in-game What's new tab explains why it's empty, with links to download PZMM and join the Discord.
- **PZMM in the main menu.** A box at the top right of the main menu opens into the latest patch notes, with a red ! when there's a new patch.
- **The game can ask PZMM for things.** From the in-game mod manager, ask PZMM to install a mod's missing requirements, back up a save, run Check setup, or open Browse Workshop or Make a mod. PZMM comes to the front on the right page, or starts if it isn't running (your browser asks to open it), and asks you first before installing or backing up.
- **PZMM notices when the game didn't load your profile.** When the game's main menu reports a different mod list than the profile you're on, PZMM says so in the activity log.
